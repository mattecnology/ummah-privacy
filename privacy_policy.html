Data Security All personal data is stored only on your device. Sensitive data (premium status) is protected with encrypted storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences). API communications are secured with HTTPS and certificate pinning. 4. Children's Privacy The app is intended for all age groups. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are filtered at G content rating level. 5. User Rights You can delete your app data at any time through your device settings. You can revoke location permission at any time. You have the right to disable notifications. 6.
